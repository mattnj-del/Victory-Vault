About This Project

Victory Vault was created as part of a class project focused on building and designing a functional website using HTML, CSS, and basic web development skills.

The goal of the assignment was to create a website centered around a topic we are passionate about. For this project, I chose sports because they bring people together across cities, teams, and leagues.

The Inspiration

Sports are more than just games. They create communities, spark conversations, and give fans a shared experience whether they are watching at home, at a stadium, or discussing games online.

Victory Vault was designed to bring multiple parts of the sports world together in one place — including live scores, sports news, merchandise, and fan discussions.

The Goal

The larger purpose of Victory Vault is to connect sports fans from different teams and leagues so they can share their opinions, celebrate victories, debate plays, and enjoy the excitement of sports together.

Whether someone is following the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, or other sports, this site aims to create a place where fans can stay updated and interact with others who share the same passion for the game.

Future Ideas

In the future, this website could grow to include real-time score updates, expanded discussion forums, personalized team tracking, and more ways for fans to interact and engage with the sports community.
